How to read these numbers

API means a collector read the number from the platform's own interface on the date shown; no outside party audited it. Hand-kept means the owner typed the number in or imported it from a platform export, and the line beside it says which. Estimate means the owner worked the number out and names the method. Headline numbers, the total and analytics are marked out of date past their age limit; imported history prints its own period, and recent posts carry no date.
