Privacy

This page lists everything this site collects, why, and for how long. It is generated from the features the site uses.

  • A count of link clicks: which link was clicked and the visitor's country only, with no IP address stored.

    Why: To see which links are useful. The counts are anonymous.

    Kept: 90 days

    This is not sensitive data.

  • A count of page views. Each view records the page, the referring site's name only (never the full address), a campaign label if the link you followed carried one (the short utm_source label: at most 30 characters of lower-case letters, digits and hyphens), and your window width as one of four sizes. From your request the server works out and keeps only: your browser and its version, your operating system, the kind of device, your language, and your country, region and city (as far as the counter is set to record them). It also marks the view as likely a person or likely automated, with a short reason, and works out a network type when it can (for example: looks like a hosting provider, a VPN or proxy, or Tor). That work is done on our own server from lists we hold, so no address is sent to anyone else. The time of a view is kept to the hour only. It also keeps a daily code made from your address and the name of the site that changes every day and cannot be turned back into the address, so one person is counted once a day on that site. This counter counts this site only. If it ever counts another site, the counts are kept separately and this page names it. Your IP address is never stored, your raw browser identification is not stored, and nothing is stored in your browser.

    Why: To see which pages are read and where visitors come from. Nothing is sent if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Totals for small groups are merged into 'other'. Nothing in these records identifies a person, so we cannot find and delete the views of one person on request; the individual records are deleted automatically after 7 days.

    Kept: Each view record is deleted after 7 days; the daily code is deleted at the end of the same day; only anonymous totals are kept longer, for up to 90 days

    This is not sensitive data.
