Privacy

This page lists everything this site collects, why, and for how long. It is generated from the features the site uses.

  • When you click a link, the site stores the link, its source label, and the hour. Labels outside the creator’s fixed list count as “other.” Missing or unusable labels count as “none.” The record has no IP address or country. Separate daily totals count clicks by source and by country. Source and country are never combined or linked to a page view or person. The site honors Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track for link clicks. If your browser sends either signal, the click is not counted or stored, and the site's own script sends nothing. If scripts are blocked or fail, your browser may still send a small request to the site. The server throws it away without storing anything about it. The link still opens normally.

    Why: These records count link clicks and show daily totals by source or country.

    Kept: Hourly records are deleted after 90 days. Daily counts are deleted after 90 days. Monthly totals are kept for up to 25 months.

    This is not sensitive data.

  • This counter counts this site only. If it ever counts another site, the counts are kept separately and this page names it. Each view records the page, referring site’s name, campaign and source labels, one of four window sizes, and the hour. It keeps browser and version, operating system, device kind, language, and the location detail enabled: country and region. It marks the view as likely human or automated and gives a short reason. Source labels outside the site’s list count as “other.” Labels use lower-case letters, digits, and hyphens, with a 30-character limit. The server creates a daily code from the address and site name. It cannot reveal the address. Short hashed counters limit flooding. The IP address and raw browser identification are never stored. The counter stores nothing in your browser.

    Why: The daily code counts one person once per day on one site. Short counters stop one client from flooding the counter. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track stop all view data from being sent. Small groups are combined as “other.”

    Kept: View records are normally deleted after 7 days. If the site has not yet added a day's records to its daily totals, the records are kept longer so the totals are not lost. After 21 days, they are due for deletion no matter what. They are removed during the next cleanup runs, which can occasionally be delayed for a short time. The daily code is deleted at the end of each day. Abuse counters are deleted within about two hours. Anonymous totals are deleted after 90 days. Records do not identify anyone, so records for a specific person cannot be deleted.

    This is not sensitive data.

Who we are

the site owner is the controller for this site. For privacy requests, contact the site owner.

What this site measures

This site measures page views and link clicks in aggregate. Measurement is designed so the site cannot tell who you are.

  • No cookies are set for measurement.
  • Your IP address is not stored.
  • A daily hash helps avoid counting one visit twice and limit abuse. It rotates and exists only for that UTC day.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the site does not record the view or click.

Raw records store location at most to the level of region. If city-level totals are enabled, they appear only in daily aggregate counters and are hidden below a minimum count.

Media-kit unlock cookie

When you unlock the media kit, the gate sets one cookie. It is HttpOnly, secure, and limited to this host. It lasts 24 hours. It is signed so the site can recognize that you unlocked the kit. It contains no analytics.

How long records are kept

  • Raw view records are kept for 7 days by default. They may remain until they are rolled into daily totals, up to 21 days.
  • Click records are kept for 90 days.
  • Daily totals are kept for 90 days.
  • Monthly totals are kept for 25 months.

Why information is processed

In GDPR terms, the legal basis for measurement is legitimate interest. The site owner has a legitimate interest in understanding which pages and links are used, keeping the site working, and limiting abuse. Consent is not used for measurement because the measurements do not identify you.

For the media-kit gate, the legal basis is the site owner’s legitimate interest in sending the kit you requested and replying to you.

Who receives information

Cloudflare provides hosting, database services, rate limiting, and the bot check.

No one else receives visitor data. The site owner does not sell or share visitor data, including the lead list.

Cloudflare is a US company. Cloudflare states that it relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses as a fallback.

Bot checks

The beacon bot check protects page-view and click measurement. It never runs for visitors whose browsers send Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

The media-kit gate form uses Cloudflare Turnstile when you use the form. This user-requested step needs a bot check. Turnstile receives your IP address, browser fingerprint signals, and user agent to distinguish people from bots.

The site may also use a small proof-of-work puzzle. It runs in your browser and needs nothing from you. Neither type of bot check is used to track you.

Your rights

You may ask what identifying information is held about you, request correction or deletion, object to processing, or ask for a copy. Where applicable, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Measurement records cannot be matched to a person. Access and deletion requests can therefore be fulfilled only for information that identifies you, such as a media-kit lead. When you request deletion, your name, email, and other details you provided are removed from the lead list, though backups may retain copies until they expire. A keyed hash of an identifier, which may include your email address, suppresses repeat owner alerts for 10 minutes and is removed when the next alert is processed or the owner clears it manually. Privacy requests receive a reply within one month.

Children

The media-kit gate is intended for business contacts. If you are a minor and entered an address, contact the site owner and ask for it to be deleted.
