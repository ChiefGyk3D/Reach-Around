Privacy

This page lists everything this site collects, why, and for how long. It is generated from the features the site uses.

  • A count of link clicks: which link was clicked and the visitor's country only, with no IP address stored.

    Why: To see which links are useful. The counts are anonymous.

    Kept: 90 days

    This is not sensitive data.

  • A count of page views. Each view records the page, the referring site's name only (never the full address), a campaign label if the link you followed carried one (the short utm_source label: at most 30 characters of lower-case letters, digits and hyphens), and your window width as one of four sizes. From your request the server works out and keeps only: your browser and operating system, the kind of device, your language, and your country, region and city (as far as the counter is set to record them). It also marks the view as likely a person or likely automated, and keeps a daily code made from your address that changes every day and cannot be turned back into the address, so one person is counted once a day. Your IP address is never stored, and nothing is stored in your browser.

    Why: To see which pages are read and where visitors come from. Nothing is sent if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

    Kept: Each view record is deleted after a few days (7 by default); the daily code is deleted by the daily cleanup; only anonymous totals are kept longer

    This is not sensitive data.
