Privacy

This page lists everything this site collects, why, and for how long. It is generated from the features the site uses.

  • A count of link clicks: which link was clicked, the visitor's country, and the short source label the link you came in on carried (from a fixed list), with the time rounded to the hour, with no IP address stored. Totals of these counts per day and per month hold no time finer than a day.

    Why: To see which links are useful. The counts are anonymous.

    Kept: 90 days for the hourly click records; the daily and monthly totals for up to 25 months

    This is not sensitive data.

  • The email address you enter to unlock the kit, so the creator can send it to you.

    Why: To deliver the kit and to contact you about it.

    Kept: 30 days

    This is not sensitive data.

  • A cookie that remembers the kit is unlocked in this browser.

    Why: So you do not have to enter your email again on a later visit.

    Kept: In your browser until it expires or you delete it

    This is not sensitive data.
