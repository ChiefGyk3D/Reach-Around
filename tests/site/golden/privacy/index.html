Privacy

This page lists everything this site collects, why, and for how long. It is generated from the features the site uses.

  • When you click a link, the site stores the link, its source label, and the hour. Labels outside the creator’s fixed list count as “other.” Missing or unusable labels count as “none.” The record has no IP address or country. Separate daily totals count clicks by source and by country. Source and country are never combined or linked to a page view or person. The site honors Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track for link clicks, just as it does for page views. If your browser sends either signal, the click is not counted. Nothing about it is sent or stored. The link still opens normally.

    Why: These records count link clicks and show daily totals by source or country.

    Kept: Hourly records are deleted after 90 days. Daily counts are deleted after 90 days. Monthly totals are kept for up to 25 months.

    This is not sensitive data.
