## Capacity

The sizes above are the maintainer's own hours. The maintainer's plan is to oversee agents that do the building, which they estimate at 15 to 40 hours a week of oversight, partly alongside a day job. So the totals below are treated as oversight hours, and the calendar is also limited by task dependencies and review turnaround, not only by hours.

**Formula.** `date = start + round(remaining_hours / hours_per_week * 7)` calendar days, computed in Python (the same rounding the first draft used; checked by reproducing its table exactly from its 2026-10-26 start).

**Assumptions.**

- Start is 2026-10-24, the `v0.1.0` date. Plan 1 is counted as done, so "remaining" is the cumulative hours minus the 106 h of Plan 1 (its actual oversight hours were not logged).
- Steady 7-day weeks at the stated rate, no gaps for waiting on the maintainer's decisions, no buffer, no allowance for review turnaround beyond what is inside the plans' estimates.
- Hours are the revised plans' hours: Plan 2 at 201 h without Task 16F, Plan 2b at 41 h, Plan 3 at 110 h, with no optional decision option taken.
- Phases 4, 5 and 6 keep their original sizes. They will probably grow when planned in detail (Plan 2 packages grew 2.3 times, Plan 3 1.6 times).
- The three new items (Phases 1b and 2c, 82 h provisional) are placed after the existing phases in the order listed, so they move no earlier milestone. The maintainer can pull any of them earlier; each item's own days are in the second table.

## Deviations from the approved spec

1. **Collectors move from phase 1 to phase 2.** They depend on the snapshot store, which the spec already places in phase 2. The spec's phase list should be reordered to match; that is a small follow-up amendment.
2. **Click tracking uses the `ping` attribute with a beacon fallback, not a redirect route.** It removes the open-redirect risk and keeps links working if the Worker is down. The spec's `event` endpoint stays; only the mechanism is named.
3. **A privacy page and cookie disclosure are in phase 1** (Task 6A), generated from the enabled features, as the maintainer asked.

## What the maintainer does, and when

- **Before Task 11:** create the Cloudflare Pages project and D1 database, a scoped API token, and the test hostname's DNS record. Exact steps are in `docs/deploy.md`.
- **Before phase 3:** a Turnstile site, a Mailgun account and sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
- **Any time:** read the terms listed as unverified in the research index (Kick, Twitch, StreamElements REST, YouTube verification).
- **Task 7:** review the design screenshots before it merges.

## Process

Every work package becomes an issue with Epic, Area, Size and Sprint on the board before work starts, and every PR links its issue. Packages are created as issues when their phase's step-level plan is approved, so the board never holds work nobody has planned.
