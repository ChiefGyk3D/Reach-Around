- Fediverse collector slice 2, the Bluesky provider (#922, design in `docs/design/fediverse-collector.md`, epic #244). New `reach_around.counts.bluesky_feed` reads one public account on `public.api.bsky.app` with no login: one `getProfile` call and up to twelve `getAuthorFeed` pages. The whole run shares one budget of 13 HTTP requests (a retry or a redirect hop draws on it like any other request) and one absolute monotonic deadline of 90 seconds that covers the pauses between calls, the retry waits and every request's own timeout; a cursor is checked and never followed from a response, and a cursor seen earlier in the run stops the walk as incomplete. The account's own posts of the 30 days ending at the run instant, by the server's `indexedAt` and never the author-supplied `createdAt`, become rows of three whole numbers (reposts, likes, replies); reposts of other accounts, replies to others, other authors and pinned posts older than the window are not counted. The feed's order is not trusted: one old post never ends the walk, which stops at the end of the cursor, at the budget (incomplete) or after one whole page of own posts that are all older than the window. A snapshot is anonymous: it holds the counts, a date, a flag and a fixed reason word, and no actor, handle, DID, link, id, text or time (which account it belongs to is config; `entry_from_snapshot` takes the actor from config to build the re-check link). A read that cannot finish returns a fixed reason word (`partial_pages`, `page_cap`, `rate_limited`, `needs_token`, `account_not_found`, `clock_skew`, `bad_shape`, `timeout`, and the others on the closed list) and keeps the prior rows; a zero is only a finished empty window. The actor is validated strictly (a handle of dot-joined letter, digit and hyphen labels, or a `did:plc:` or `did:web:` id). Also added: `fetch_json_view` (the object twin of `fetch_json_array`, with the same strict numbers and header view), the guard pass over the snapshot (recursive over every key and leaf at any depth, then an exact schema; a hit writes nothing), the window sums for the history store (`posts_30d`, `boosts_30d`, `favorites_30d`, `replies_30d`, complete reads only) and `entry_from_snapshot`, which turns a fresh complete snapshot into the same `FediverseEntry` the hand-kept `kit.fediverse` block takes; the hand-kept path is unchanged and the loader wiring is slice 4. No egress change: `public.api.bsky.app` was already on the list. Hermetic tests only.
