- Fediverse collector slice 2, the Bluesky provider (#922, design in `docs/design/fediverse-collector.md`, epic #244). New `reach_around.counts.bluesky_feed` reads one public account on `public.api.bsky.app` with no login: one `getProfile` call and up to twelve `getAuthorFeed` pages (13 calls at most, a pause between calls, 90 seconds for the whole run, a cursor that is checked and never followed from a response). The account's own posts of the 30 days ending at the run instant, by the server's `indexedAt` and never the author-supplied `createdAt`, become rows of three whole numbers (reposts, likes, replies); reposts of other accounts, replies to others, other authors and pinned posts older than the window are not counted, and no id, handle, text or time leaves the provider. A read that cannot finish returns a fixed reason word (`partial_pages`, `page_cap`, `rate_limited`, `needs_token`, `account_not_found`, `clock_skew`, `bad_shape`, `timeout`, and the others on the closed list) and keeps the prior rows; a zero is only a finished empty window. The actor is validated strictly (a handle of dot-joined letter, digit and hyphen labels, or a `did:plc:` or `did:web:` id). Also added: `fetch_json_view` (the object twin of `fetch_json_array`, with the same strict numbers and header view), the guard pass over the snapshot (the import guard runs over every key and string, a hit writes nothing), the window sums for the history store (`posts_30d`, `boosts_30d`, `favorites_30d`, `replies_30d`, complete reads only) and `entry_from_snapshot`, which turns a fresh complete snapshot into the same `FediverseEntry` the hand-kept `kit.fediverse` block takes; the hand-kept path is unchanged and the loader wiring is slice 4. No egress change: `public.api.bsky.app` was already on the list. Hermetic tests only.
