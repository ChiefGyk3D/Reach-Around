- Link page v2 main screen, the compact amber grid (#873, epic #509): new `page.density: compact` for the smart stack draws every link as an icon-over-label tile in named, always open groups (Live now with Twitch, Kick and YouTube Live in the same tile, Videos, Follow, Chat, Support, a gear group and More), so the whole page fits a phone screen with no scrolling at 390x844, 390x664 and 360x640 (tiles shrink to the 44 px tap size instead). A tab may set `columns` (1 to 6) and the last short row stretches. `featured`, `collapsed`, `spoke` and `style` are refused with it, new intents `gear`, `more` and `hire`, and seven new original icons (`facebook`, `kofi`, `telegram`, `website`, `radio`, `shield`, `tools`). The default `comfortable` density and every existing layout are unchanged. Schema, `docs/config.md`, `docs/design.md` and `examples/instance/site-link-grid.yaml` updated; tests in `tests/config/test_smart_stack_compact_config.py`, `tests/site/test_smart_stack_compact.py` and `tests/site/test_smart_stack_compact_visual.py`. A group whose links are all outside their `show_from`/`show_until` window is dropped (no build crash), a label is clamped to two lines inside its tile, and a tile is at least 44 px wide as well as tall (a row of four to six falls back to three across on a narrow screen, then two and one across below that, down to a 240 px window). The Support and Contact screens are a follow-up. The schedule line is kept on short screens (the tiles give a line back and the page may scroll a little if it wraps), and desktop groups sit in DOM and keyboard order with no dense reordering. The no-sideways-scroll guarantee is stated for a 240 px wide screen and up (a 44 px tile cannot fit below that) and tested at 240, 280 and 320 px and in a 48 px group, and the Watch group refuses `columns` (its count is automatic). Recommendation groups: a new `services` intent draws a "Services I Use" group beside "Gear I Use", so recommendations are two groups instead of one flat list. `affiliate` on a link is now a strict boolean (`yes`, `"true"` and `1` are refused instead of silently reading as unflagged), and a title of only spaces is refused. A link marked `affiliate: true` carries `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`, and the page shows the `disclosure` line if and only if a visible flagged link is on it. The grid example now has both groups, with a flagged placeholder gear item and a flagged referral link as the first services item, and its Support group is one row of five so the page still fits a phone screen; tests in `tests/site/test_link_groups.py`.
