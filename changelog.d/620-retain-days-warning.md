- History store: warn before a platform's retention window ends (#620, epic #364, follow-up to #586). New `numbers.retain_days` (platform to days, 1 to 3650, strict whole numbers) and `numbers.retain_warn_days` (1 to 365, default 7; each window must be larger). With a history file, `counts refresh` prints one count-only `retention warning` line per listed platform whose stored `api` rows are within the margin of the window or past it. Hand-kept and imported rows are not counted, no value is printed, and nothing stored or deleted changes. Schema and `docs/config.md` regenerated. Tests in `tests/test_counts_retain_days.py`. #619 (workflow tombstone rewrite) is not part of this change.
