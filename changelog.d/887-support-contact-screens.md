- Link page v2 Support and Contact screens (#887, epic #509): a tab may set `screen: support` or `screen: contact` to draw its group on its own page (`/support/`, `/contact/`) behind a tab bar, with new intents `free`, `paid`, `crypto` and `contact`, a one-line `note` under a heading, and a `hire` group that is exactly one link drawn as a separate panel with one amber button. The Support screen takes `free`, `paid`, `crypto` in that order and the Contact screen `contact`, `hire` (any other intent or order is refused). The crypto group links inside the site only, and the finished page is checked for that. A screen with no visible link is not built or shown in the tab bar. The gear section is named Gear I Use in the examples (`examples/instance/site-link-screens.yaml`). A screen refusal names the tab by its place in the list (tab #3), never by its id or title, and the build stops with a fixed-reason error when a visible `tab:` link points at a screen group that the date windows left with no visible link, instead of writing a dead link.
