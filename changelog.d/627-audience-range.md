- Kit audience summary as a range (#627, epic #244): the "Combined audience" block is replaced by "Audience across platforms", which prints the largest single follows number to the sum of follows (for example "3,400 to 4,600"), the platform and number counts, the largest single audience with its platform (a tie names every tied platform, Twitch and Kick alike), the overlap sentence whenever more than one platform contributes, and a caveat that follower counts include inactive and automated accounts. The oldest date, the weakest source label, the out-of-date mark and the before-and-after Reach Around note stay. No output prints one deduplicated audience number. Discord and Matrix members are never added into either bound: they print on their own line ("Community members: ...") with a note that they are chat members, not followers (`unified_total` now skips them, so a kit that counted them before shows a smaller total). New optional `kit.audience_summary` (`poll` with `respondents` of at least 5, `as_of`, `avg_other_platforms`; and `show_estimate`, off by default): with both, one labeled line "Estimate from a poll of N people on DATE: roughly X unique people" prints beside the range, X to two significant figures, with its method and a note that the poll is hand-kept; it is left out when it falls outside the range. Both PDFs print the block and their text check expects each new sentence. Schema, the instance renderer vocabulary, goldens and docs (`docs/kit.md`, `docs/config.md`) updated.
