- Owner report (#397): the private analytics block now states plainly "not available" for Twitch and Kick, with identical wording, where no views, watch time or demographics figure is collected. It also appears in the JSON as `not_available`; the CSV is unchanged. Platform identifiers are canonicalized once, for both platforms alike: NFKC-normalized, then a name, a leading @ handle, a bare host, host:port, `//host` or `scheme://host` (www or m subdomain, one trailing dot, path, query or fragment allowed) reduces to one platform by exact host (a lookalike host such as `nottwitch.tv` is never matched). Malformed authorities (an empty, non-numeric or out-of-range port, userinfo, brackets, percent-encoding, backslashes, a scheme without `//`) are counted as unrecognized and never shown. Non-ASCII lookalikes are counted as unrecognized and not shown. A legacy block without `not_available` still renders.
