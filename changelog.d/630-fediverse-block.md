The platform card then says its counts are as reported by the server, prints a re-check link to the profile beside every count, works out the per-post medians (median first; the mean only beside it and only with `show_mean: true`), prints a value that was not typed as "not provided" and never zero, and states what each platform does not expose (impressions, reach and demographics; Pixelfed also any insights). It is hand-kept: nothing is fetched, and the collector for public post data gets its own design issue before any collector code. The open group's default set lists the new keys.
